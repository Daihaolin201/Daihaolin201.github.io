Research Experience

Bayesian Dual Control for Cooperative Multi-Robot Source Seeking
Sept. 2024 – Present
The University of Manchester · PhD Researcher
  • Built MATLAB simulations of Bayesian dual control for cooperative multi-robot source seeking, with posterior-mean consensus fusion and safety-constrained action projection.
  • Across 30 random seeds and ablation tests, compared packet counts and RMSE for trace-normalised event-triggered communication and inverse-trace fusion under heterogeneous sensing and communication stress.
  • Presented algorithm design and simulation results at the EEE Postgraduate Research Conference poster session.
  • First-authored papers accepted at CCC 2026 and IAI 2026; final manuscripts submitted. The IAI paper was named a Best Paper Award Finalist.
