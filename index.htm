Research

Bayesian Dual Control for Cooperative Multi-Robot Source Seeking
Sept. 2024 – Present
The University of Manchester · PhD Researcher
  • I study how teams of robots use noisy measurements to locate an unknown source and balance gathering information with progress towards the source.
  • My work combines Bayesian estimation, distributed control and multi-robot information sharing, with evaluation in MATLAB simulations.
