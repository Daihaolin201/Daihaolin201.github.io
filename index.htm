Bayesian Dual Control for Cooperative Multi-Robot Source Seeking
Sept. 2024 – Present
The University of Manchester · PhD Researcher

  • Presented algorithm design and simulation results at the EEE Postgraduate Research Conference poster session.
  • First-authored papers accepted at CCC 2026 and IAI 2026; final manuscripts submitted. The IAI paper was named a Best Paper Award Finalist.
